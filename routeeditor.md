# routeeditor

The **routeeditor** feature allows dispatchers to manage route details and survey configurations. Use this tool to adjust route schedules, track survey responses, view logs, and manage vehicle photos. You will achieve complete control over route settings and driver inspection data.

### Getting Started

#### Requirements
- Active user account in **Nomadia Delivery**.
- Access to the **machines** module.

#### Setup Steps
1. Log into **Nomadia Delivery**.

![Nomadia Delivery Login – Accessing machines and scrolling down to the route section.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_07_to_0_to_14.gif "Nomadia Delivery Login – Accessing machines and scrolling down to the route section.")

2. Go to **machines** and scroll down to access the **route section**.

![Route Section – Viewing delivery names and accessing edit icons.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_14.png "Route Section – Viewing delivery names and accessing edit icons.")

3. Click the **edit icon** next to the **delivery name**.

![Route Editor Interface – Opening the route editor details panel.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_14_to_0_to_21.gif "Route Editor Interface – Opening the route editor details panel.")

### Feature Overview

- **Date**: Sets the scheduled execution date for the route.

![Date Field – Setting the scheduled route date.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_28.png "Date Field – Setting the scheduled route date.")

- **Start hour**: Defines the official daily start time for the route.

![Start Hour Field – Defining the official start hour.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_33.png "Start Hour Field – Defining the official start hour.")

- **Delivery name**: Displays the locked delivery identifier for assigned routes.

![Delivery Name Field – Viewing the assigned delivery name.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_42.png "Delivery Name Field – Viewing the assigned delivery name.")

- **Departure allowed**: Controls whether departure permissions block, grant, or disable blocking.

![Departure Allowed Dropdown – Selecting departure permission rules.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_49.png "Departure Allowed Dropdown – Selecting departure permission rules.")

- **Vehicle usage**: Configures operational rules and settings for assigned vehicles.

![Vehicle Usage Field – Configuring vehicle operational settings.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_57.png "Vehicle Usage Field – Configuring vehicle operational settings.")

- **Survey responses**: Displays vehicle inspection survey details submitted from mobile devices.

![Survey Responses Tab – Viewing mobile survey submissions.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_04.png "Survey Responses Tab – Viewing mobile survey submissions.")

- **Configuration**: Enables survey response capture and response storage settings.

![Configuration Tab – Setting up survey response capture.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_42.png "Configuration Tab – Setting up survey response capture.")

- **Logs**: Shows a detailed audit history of all route modifications.

![Logs Tab – Auditing route modification history.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_22.png "Logs Tab – Auditing route modification history.")

- **Photos**: Loads vehicle photos and manages sample calculations.

![Photos Tab – Managing vehicle inspection photos.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_33.png "Photos Tab – Managing vehicle inspection photos.")

- **Route sheet**: Downloads the printable route summary sheet.

![Route Sheet Button – Downloading the route document.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_48.png "Route Sheet Button – Downloading the route document.")

- **Save**: Applies and stores all route modifications permanently.

![Save Button – Applying all route changes.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_54.png "Save Button – Applying all route changes.")

### How To: Modify Route Details

1. Click the **edit icon** next to the **delivery name**.

![Route Edit Trigger – Opening the edit details panel.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_14_to_0_to_21.gif "Route Edit Trigger – Opening the edit details panel.")

2. Update the **date** and **start hour** fields.

![Date and Hour Adjustment – Setting route date and start time.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_28_to_0_to_33.gif "Date and Hour Adjustment – Setting route date and start time.")

3. Select **departure allowed** permissions to set blocking rules.

![Departure Selection – Choosing departure permission mode.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_49.png "Departure Selection – Choosing departure permission mode.")

4. Adjust **vehicle usage** settings as needed.

![Vehicle Usage Setup – Updating vehicle usage options.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_57.png "Vehicle Usage Setup – Updating vehicle usage options.")

5. Click **Save** to apply modifications.

![Save Modifications – Confirming updated route settings.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_54.png "Save Modifications – Confirming updated route settings.")

### How To: Configure and View Vehicle Surveys

1. Click **Configuration** to open survey options.

![Configuration Selection – Opening initial survey configuration.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_42_to_1_to_47.gif "Configuration Selection – Opening initial survey configuration.")

2. Click **Configure the survey**.

![Configure Survey Action – Accessing survey configuration list.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_51_to_1_to_54.gif "Configure Survey Action – Accessing survey configuration list.")

3. Click the **pincer icon** next to **vehicle checking survey**.

![Pincer Icon Click – Editing vehicle checking survey settings.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_58_to_2_to_02.gif "Pincer Icon Click – Editing vehicle checking survey settings.")

4. Enable **store all responses** and **capture** options.

![Store Responses Check – Enabling response storage and capture.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_02_to_2_to_10.gif "Store Responses Check – Enabling response storage and capture.")

5. Click **Save** to store survey settings.

![Save Survey Settings – Saving survey configuration.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_10.png "Save Survey Settings – Saving survey configuration.")

6. Click **Survey responses** in the top left corner.

![Survey Responses Tab – Navigating to survey responses.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_04_to_1_to_09.gif "Survey Responses Tab – Navigating to survey responses.")

7. Select **Vehicle checking survey** and click **Load**.

![Load Survey Details – Loading driver survey data.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_1_to_09_to_1_to_22.gif "Load Survey Details – Loading driver survey data.")

### How To: View Route Modification Logs

![Logs Tab Selection – Opening route change logs.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_17_to_2_to_22.gif "Logs Tab Selection – Opening route change logs.")

### How To: Manage Vehicle Photos and Samples

1. Click **Photos** to open photo settings.

![Photos Tab Selection – Accessing vehicle photo panel.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_33_to_2_to_40.gif "Photos Tab Selection – Accessing vehicle photo panel.")

2. Click **Customize the list** at the top.

![Customize List Click – Opening display field options.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_44_to_2_to_52.gif "Customize List Click – Opening display field options.")

3. Select fields and click the **right arrow** to display them.

![Right Arrow Action – Moving selected fields to display list.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_2_to_52_to_3_to_05.gif "Right Arrow Action – Moving selected fields to display list.")

4. Select the photo and choose the **type of sample**.

![Sample Type Selection – Assigning sample type to vehicle photo.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_05_to_3_to_15.gif "Sample Type Selection – Assigning sample type to vehicle photo.")

5. Click **Save** and return to view calculations.

![Save Photos – Applying photo and sample configuration.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_15_to_3_to_28.gif "Save Photos – Applying photo and sample configuration.")

### How To: Download Route Sheets

1. Click **Route sheet** to generate the document.

![Route Sheet Click – Generating printable route sheet.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_3_to_48_to_3_to_54.gif "Route Sheet Click – Generating printable route sheet.")

2. Save the downloaded file to your local computer.

#### Troubleshooting
If the **delivery name** field is locked, check the route assignment status. System status prevents modifications to assigned routes.

![Locked Fields Warning – Viewing locked assigned route options.]({IMAGE_BASE_PATH}routeeditor-routeeditor_timestamp_0_to_33_to_0_to_42.gif "Locked Fields Warning – Viewing locked assigned route options.")

### Productivity Tips

- 💡 **Survey Pre-configuration**: Configure survey settings initially so mobile submissions appear correctly in the survey viewer.
- ⚠️ **Assigned Route Edits**: Avoid trying to rename assigned routes because assigned status locks the delivery name field.
- 💡 **Audit Tracking**: Review the logs tab to verify all past route edits and maintain operational accountability.
- 💡 **Custom Field Views**: Use the right arrow during list customization to control which sample fields display.
