# Nomadbot

The **Nomadbot** feature in **Nomadia Delivery** provides an automated virtual assistant for dispatchers and planners. You can select predefined questions or type custom queries directly in the typing window. This helps you resolve operational questions quickly without leaving the platform.

## Before you begin

Ensure you meet all system requirements before accessing the feature.

* Active **Nomadia Delivery** account with dispatcher or planner permissions.
* Supported web browser with an active internet connection.

1. Log in to **Nomadia Delivery**.
2. Locate the top-right navigation bar.

![Locate the top right navigation bar](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_BeforeBegin_001.png)

3. Click the **Chatbot icon**.

![Click the Chatbot icon](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_BeforeBegin_002.gif)

## Feature overview

* **Chatbot icon**: Opens the virtual assistant interface from the top navigation bar.
* **Predefined Questions**: List of ready-to-use questions and answers for quick self-service.
* **Typing Window**: Input field where you enter custom questions.
* **Send button**: Submits your typed question to the virtual assistant.

## Access predefined questions

1. Click the **Chatbot icon** in the top right header.

![Click the Chatbot icon in the top right header](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_BeforeBegin_002.gif)

2. Review the listed **Predefined Questions and Answers**.

![Review the listed Predefined Questions and Answers](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AccessPredefinedQuestions_001.gif)

## Ask a custom question

1. Click inside the **Typing Window**.

![Click inside the Typing Window](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AskCustomQuestion_001.gif)

2. Type your question into the **Typing Window**.

![Type your question into the Typing Window](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AskCustomQuestion_002.gif)

3. Click **Send**.

![Click Send](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AskCustomQuestion_003.gif)

4. Watch the **Typing Indicator** while the answer is being generated.

![Observe the Typing Indicator while the response generates](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AskCustomQuestion_004.gif)

5. Read the answer that appears.

![Read the displayed answer based on your question](.gitbook/assets/Nomadbot_AskCustomQuestion_005.gif)
