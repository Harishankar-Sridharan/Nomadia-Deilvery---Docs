# Optimized Route Plan for Sub Contractors

As a subcontractor, you now have enhanced flexibility to plan and manage your missions or deliveries. This improvement allows subcontractors to independently optimize their routes with the help of our advanced algorithm, minimizing reliance on transporter instructions.

## Before you begin

Before subcontractors can create optimized routes, they must first be granted authorization by the primary transporter. The transporter assigns the necessary permissions as follows:

1. Navigate to the **Configuration** module.
2. Open the **Manage User Profile** section.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-18 (4).png>)

3. Update the subcontractor's access by enabling **Vehicles** and **Optimization** rights.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-19 (4).png>)

4. Once these permissions are granted, subcontractors can log in and use the route optimization features.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Nomadia_Layout_V1 (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Feature overview

* **Vehicles** and **Optimization** rights: Permissions the transporter enables in **Manage User Profile** so subcontractors can use route optimization.
* **Optimize**: Runs the optimization for the selected missions, based on the date range, team, and vehicles you define.
* **Stop**: Ends the optimization when you are satisfied with the outcome so you can see the result.
* **Display the simulation**: Lets you analyze the optimization results.
* **Validate routes**: Publishes the results directly without reviewing them.
* **Publish to the mobile app**: Shares the validated route with the driver.
* **Force**: Validates the route even when conflicts are listed.

## Run optimization with subcontractor login

1. Log in to the Nomadia Delivery application with the subcontractor account.
2. Go to the **Missions** tab.
3. Select the missions to optimize, click the **Actions** button, and choose **Optimize**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-21 (4).png>)

4. Define the optimization scope by selecting date range, team, and vehicles, then click **Optimize**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-22 (4).png>)

5. When satisfied with the outcome, click **Stop** and see the result.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-23 (4).png>)

6. Review the optimization summary that is displayed. Click **Display the simulation** to analyze results.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-24 (4).png>)

Alternatively, click **Validate routes** to publish results directly without reviewing them.

## Validate and publish routes

1. In the **Routes** panel, select the routes to validate.
2. Open the **Actions** menu, then click **Validate**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-25 (4).png>)

3. Confirm by clicking **OK**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-26 (3).png>)

4. Review the confirmation notification that appears. The route is validated.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-27 (3).png>)

5. Activate **Publish to the mobile app** at the bottom of the popup if you want to share the route with the driver.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-28 (3).png>)

6. If conflicts are listed, review them. To proceed anyway, click **Force** to validate.

### Result

The validated route is published to the mobile app.
