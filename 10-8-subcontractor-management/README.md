# Subcontractor Management

The subcontractor management feature allows transporters to delegate logistics tasks to subcontractors, making collaborations smoother and increasing operational flexibility. This feature ensures subcontractors are managed efficiently so that logistics operations remain optimized and aligned with business objectives.

In the Nomadia Delivery application, subcontractors must be registered by the transporter before they can upload mission data. To do this, the transporter needs to add the subcontractor through the Subcontractor module.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** as a transporter with a user profile that has access to the **Subcontractors** module and the **Configuration** module.
* Register each subcontractor before they upload mission data, using the **Subcontractors** module.

## Feature overview

* **Register a Subcontractor**: Adds a subcontractor to the delivery management system so that transporters can delegate logistics tasks to them.
* **Create a Subcontractor User**: Creates planner and driver users for a subcontractor so they can log in and carry out their tasks.
* **Assigning missions to subcontractor**: Allocates selected missions to a subcontractor and filters the missions assigned to each one.
* **Optimized Route Plan for Sub Contractors**: Lets subcontractors optimize, validate, and publish their own routes after the transporter grants the required rights.
