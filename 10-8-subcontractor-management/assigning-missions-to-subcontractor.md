# Assigning missions to subcontractor

Transporters can assign predefined geographic zones, based on postal codes, to subcontractors. This targeted mission allocation ensures that each subcontractor is responsible for specific areas, enhancing the efficiency of logistics operations. With just a few clicks, transporters can delegate specific missions to subcontractors, reducing manual coordination and streamlining the overall assignment process.

## Before you begin

* The new subcontractor user can access the application and perform their tasks, such as importing missions, planning, and executing missions.

## Assign missions to a subcontractor

1. Open the Nomadia Delivery application and go to the **Missions** tab.
2. Select the missions you want to assign from the mission table.
3. Click the **Actions** button and choose **Assign to subcontractor**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-13 (4).png>)

4. Select the desired subcontractor from the list to allocate the missions.
5. Click **Apply**.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-14 (4).png>)

### Result

The selected missions are assigned to the chosen subcontractor.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-15 (4).png>)

## Filter missions assigned to subcontractors

1. Open the Nomadia Delivery application and go to the **Missions** tab. By default, transporters can see all missions created in the delivery management system.
2. Click the **Prefilter** option in the mission tab.
3. Select the desired subcontractor in the **Allotted to** drop-down.
4. Click **Apply** to display the missions assigned to the selected subcontractor.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-16 (4).png>)

### Result

The mission tab shows only the missions allocated to the chosen subcontractor.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-17 (4).png>)
