# Register a Subcontractor

Register a subcontractor so that transporters can delegate logistics tasks to them. After registration, the transporter can create subcontractor access from the Configuration module. This enables subcontractors to log in to the system and carry out their tasks.

## Before you begin

* Ensure the user has the necessary rights to create and modify subcontractors.

## Feature overview

The following fields are mandatory when adding a subcontractor:

* **Identifier**
* **Subcontractor name**
* **Subcontractor address**
* **Mission types they can handle**
* **Allocated web users**
* **Allocated mobile users**
* **Profiles**
* **Agency**

## Register a subcontractor

1. Open the Nomadia Delivery application and go to the **Subcontractors** module.
2. Click the **Actions** menu and select the **Add** button.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-1 (6).png>)

3. Enter the mandatory fields. The transporter may also enter additional details such as zone and authorizations. Once the subcontractor's address is entered, the map automatically highlights their geographical location.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-2 (6).png>)

4. Click **Save** to add the subcontractor to the delivery management system.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-3 (6).png>)

### Result

A notification confirms that the subcontractor has been successfully added.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/image-4 (6).png>)
