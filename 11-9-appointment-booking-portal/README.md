# Booking Portal

Use the booking portal to let customers book and reschedule delivery slots. The pages in this section cover each part of the portal.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to the **Configuration** tab.
* Review the pages below to decide which parts of the portal you need to set up.

## Feature overview

* **Booking slots**: Embeds an I-Frame Booking Portal into contractor websites so customers can book optimized delivery slots.
* **Configure the Portal**: Defines the time slots available for booking in the appointment portal and reserves slots for missions.
* **Reschedule Configuration**: Enables end customers to reschedule their delivery or pickup missions from customer emails.
* **Rescheduling by Customer**: Lets end customers receive email notifications about their delivery and reschedule a mission to a slot that suits them.
* **Reschedule a Pre-Booked Slot Multiple Times**: Lets dispatchers pre-book and reschedule time slots multiple times using the original booking link.
