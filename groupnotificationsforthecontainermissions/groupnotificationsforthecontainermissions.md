# groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions

Group notifications for container missions aggregate alerts for several machines simultaneously. Dispatchers use this feature to streamline communications with contractors or other recipients during missions. This setup ensures automated, reliable status updates are delivered directly to stakeholders based on scanning milestones.

### Getting Started

To use this feature, make sure the following requirements are met:
* Set child-parent container and child container relationships.
* Verify that a contractor or contract is set up for each machine.

Follow these initial steps to access the settings:

1. Navigate to the **Configuration** screen.

![Frame at 0:35](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_0_to_35.png "Configuration Screen – Accessing configuration settings.")

2. Select **Container Types**.

![Frame at 0:45](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_0_to_45.png "Container Types – Selecting container types to configure.")

3. Scroll down to find the correct identifier.

![Frame at 0:51](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_0_to_51.png "Identifier Selection – Finding the container identifier to modify.")

4. Enable the **Can Aggregate Several Machines** toggle.

![Flow 0:51 to 1:03](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_0_to_51_to_1_to_03.gif "Can Aggregate Several Machines – Enabling aggregate option for multiple machines.")

### Feature Overview

* **Can Aggregate Several Machines**: Combines notifications for multiple machines. This allows sending unified group updates.

  ![Frame at 1:03](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_03.png "Aggregate Toggle – The enabled aggregate machines toggle.")

* **Customer Messages**: Section containing recipient and message configurations. This controls your notification preferences.

  ![Frame at 1:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_17.png "Customer Messages Section – Configuring recipient message options.")

* **Recipient**: Selects the recipient for notifications. This defines who receives the alerts.

  ![Frame at 1:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_17.png "Recipient Field – Selecting the message recipient.")

* **Message Type**: Selects the notification channel. You can choose either SMS or email.

  ![Frame at 1:25](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_25.png "Message Type – Dropdown menu with SMS and email options.")

* **Message Template**: Selects a pre-created message layout. This formats your notification text.

  ![Frame at 1:35](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_35.png "Message Template – Choosing a preconfigured message layout.")

* **Status**: Field to select the target status for triggering the message. This defines the status transition.

  ![Frame at 1:35](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_35.png "Status Field – Selecting the target status trigger.")

* **Notification Configuration Type**: Determines whether to send notifications after the first or last scan. This customizes alert timing.

  ![Frame at 1:44](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_44.png "Notification Type – Choice between first scan or last scan.")

* **Other Recipient Email**: Input field to add extra email addresses. This sends notifications to additional stakeholders.

  ![Frame at 2:01](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_01.png "Other Recipient – Adding extra notification receivers.")

* **Save Button**: Commits all your selected configuration changes. This stores your active preferences.

  ![Frame at 2:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_17.png "Save Button – Committing the container notification settings.")

### How To: Configure Group Notifications

1. Scroll down to the **Customer Messages** section.

![Frame at 1:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_17.png "Customer Messages – Navigating to the customer messages section.")

2. Select a **Recipient**.

![Frame at 1:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_17.png "Recipient Selection – Selecting the notification receiver.")

3. Choose SMS or email in **Message Type**.

![Frame at 1:25](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_25.png "Message Type Selection – Choosing SMS or email channel.")

4. Select your pre-created **Message Template**.

![Frame at 1:35](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_35.png "Template Selection – Choosing a pre-created template.")

5. Choose the target status to change to in **Status**.

![Frame at 1:35](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_35.png "Status Selection – Selecting the status to change to.")

6. Select the **Notification Configuration Type**.

![Frame at 1:44](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_1_to_44.png "Configuration Type – Select timing for first or last scan.")

7. Enter additional emails in **Other Recipient Email**.

![Frame at 2:01](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_01.png "Enter Email – Adding other recipient email addresses.")

8. Click **Save** to complete setup.

![Frame at 2:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_17.png "Save Setup – Click save to finalize notification configurations.")

### How To: Trigger Group Notifications

1. Go to the **Machines** screen.

![Frame at 2:17](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_17.png "Machines Screen – Navigating to machines screen.")

2. Click the **Pencil Icon** (or **Printer Icon**) next to your container type.

![Frame at 2:34](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_34.png "Edit Icon – Clicking edit icon to modify container type.")

3. Change the status from **Created** to a different status.

![Flow 2:34 to 2:44](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_34_to_2_to_44.gif "Change Status – Changing container status.")

4. Click **Save** to send the notification.

![Frame at 2:45](groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions-groupnotificationsforthecontainermissions_timestamp_2_to_45.png "Save Machine – Saving the modified status to send alerts.")

### Productivity Tips

* **Child-Parent Relationships**: Pre-configuring child-parent relationships ensures grouped notifications work correctly.
* **Missing Contracts**: Avoid editing machine status before sending contracts as notifications will fail to deliver.
* **Scan Timing Selection**: Choosing first or last scan delivery optimizes the notification timing based on customer needs.
* **Save Action**: Avoid leaving the machine page without saving or the status change will not trigger notifications.
