# Customize the Constraints

Choose which constraint fields to display in the vehicles table.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to the **Configuration** page.
* Make sure a fleet exists under **Manage the Vehicles** in **My Data**.

## Feature overview

* **Manage the Vehicles**: The option under **My Data** that lists your vehicle fleets.
* **Actions**: The menu you use to add and open **Constraints**.
* **Constraints**: The fields you can choose to display on the vehicles table.

## Customize the constraints

1. Go to **Configuration**.
2. Click the **Configuration** menu.
3. Under My Data, click **Manage the Vehicles**.
4. Click the desired name.
5. Click **Actions**.
6. Click **Add**.
7. Click **Constraints**.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-50.png)

8. Choose which fields you want to display on the table.
9. Click **Save**.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-51.png)

### Result

The fields are displayed successfully.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-52.png)
