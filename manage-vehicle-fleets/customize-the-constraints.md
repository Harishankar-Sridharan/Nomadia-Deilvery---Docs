# Customize the Constraints

Choose which constraint fields to display in the vehicles table.

## Customize the constraints

1. Go to **Configuration**.
2. Click the **Configuration** menu.
3. Under My Data, click **Manage the Vehicles**.
4. Click the desired name.
5. Click **Actions**.
6. Click **Add**.
7. Click **Constraints**.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-50.png)

8. Choose which fields you want to display on the table.
9. Click **Save**.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-51.png)

### Result

The fields are displayed successfully.

![](../.gitbook/assets/image-52.png)

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Field selection**: Avoid selecting too many fields at once, as it may become difficult to read or navigate.
{% endhint %}
