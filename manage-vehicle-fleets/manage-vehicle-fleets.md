# Manage Vehicle Fleets

The Manage Vehicle Fleets in Nomadia Delivery allows administrators and planners to maintain an up-to-date registry of the vehicles used for delivery operations. This includes adding, editing, or deactivating vehicles, assigning specific characteristics, and ensuring that each vehicle is properly configured to meet logistical needs.

## Before you begin

* Access to the **Configuration** menu in **Nomadia Delivery**, where the vehicle fleets are managed under **My Data**.

## Import Vehicles fleets

1. Go to Configuration.

![Go to Configuration](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_001.png)

2. Click on Configuration menu
3. Under My Data, click on Manage the Vehicles.

![Under My Data, click on Manage the Vehicles](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_002.png)

4. Under My Fleets, click the Actions dropdown menu.
5. Click on Import

![Click on Import](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_003.png)

6. Click on Browse File to upload the file

![Click on Browse File to upload the file](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_004.png)

7. Select a Valid file from your local system.

![Select a Valid file from your local system](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_005.png)

### Result

The vehicle fleets have been imported successfully.

![The vehicle fleets have been imported successfully](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_ImportVehiclesFleets_006.png)

## Add a Vehicles fleets

1. Go to Configuration.
2. Click on Configuration Menu
3. Under My Data, click on Manage the Vehicles.
4. Under My Fleets, click the Actions dropdown menu.
5. Click on Add

![Click on Add](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_AddVehiclesFleets_001.png)

6. Enter the Name and Agency name

When adding a vehicle, enable the **Dedicated start address** toggle to edit the **Start address.**&#x20;

7. Click on Add

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_AddVehiclesFleets_002.png" alt="Click on Add"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

The address field have been enabled to modify it.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_AddVehiclesFleets_003.png" alt="The address field have been enabled to modify it"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The vehicle fleets have been added successfully.

![The vehicle fleets have been added successfully](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_AddVehiclesFleets_004.png)

## Delete a Vehicles fleets

1. Go to Configuration.
2. Click on Configuration Menu
3. Under My Data, click on Manage the Vehicles.
4. Select a Team
5. Under My Fleets, click the Actions dropdown menu.
6. Click on Delete

![Click on Delete](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_DeleteVehiclesFleets_001.png)

7. You will see a confirmation pop-up message stating: "Do you want to delete the Vehicle?"
8. Click on Delete

![Click on Delete](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_DeleteVehiclesFleets_002.png)

### Result

The Vehicle fleets have been deleted successfully.

![The Vehicle fleets have been deleted successfully](../.gitbook/assets/VehicleFleets_DeleteVehiclesFleets_003.png)
