# Manage Vehicle Fleets

Use the pages in this section to manage the vehicles and fleets in Nomadia Delivery.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to the **Configuration** page.
* Open **Manage the Vehicles** under **My Data** to reach your fleets and vehicles.

## Feature overview

* **Manage Vehicle Fleets**: Import, add, and delete vehicle fleets.
* **Manage Vehicles in a fleet**: Add, delete, export, color, and customize the display of the vehicles in a fleet.
* **Customize the Constraints**: Choose which constraint fields to display in the vehicles table.
