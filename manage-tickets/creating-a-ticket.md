# Creating a Ticket

Use tickets to report and resolve mission-related queries between contractors and transporters. This page covers creating, replying to, closing, reopening, and bulk editing tickets.

## Create a ticket

A contractor user follows these steps to create a ticket.

1. Open the Nomadia Delivery application and go to the **Missions** tab.
2. Apply the Not delivered / Not picked pre-filter to display missions that are in distress.
3. Click the **pencil** icon next to the relevant mission to switch to edit mode.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Open the **Help** tab and select **Create a ticket** to initiate a support ticket for the mission.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Enter your query in the provided field and click **Send** to submit the ticket to the transporter.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

The ticket is created, and the transporter receives an email notification about the reported issue.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Reply to a ticket as a transporter

1. Open the email that the transporter receives once a contractor creates a ticket. It contains the query and a direct link to the related mission.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (13).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click the **link** to open the Mission Editor and view the ticket details.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Alternatively, locate missions with active tickets by applying the Open ticket filter = Yes in the **Missions** tab and saving it as a pre-filter for ongoing monitoring.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. To respond, open the **Help** tab, enter your reply in the message box, and click **Reply** to send the response.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Reply to a ticket as a contractor

1. Open the email that the contractor receives when the transporter replies to a ticket. It contains the response and a direct link to the related mission.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. To reply, open the Mission Editor, go to the **Help** tab, type your message in the response box, and click **Reply**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Close a ticket

The transporter follows these steps to close a ticket.

1. After sharing the required information with the contractor, select the **Close Ticket** button in the Mission Editor.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Reopen a ticket

The contractor follows these steps to reopen a ticket.

1. Open the email that is sent to the contractor as soon as the transporter closes the ticket.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click the **link** and reopen the ticket if the resolution provided by the transporter is not satisfactory.
3. In the **Help** tab of the mission editor, click the **reopen** button to reopen the ticket if the response is not satisfactory.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Bulk edit tickets

Contractors or transporters follow these steps to perform a bulk edit of tickets.

1. Go to the **Tickets** module to fetch the list of the tickets created in the system.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Select one or many tickets and click **Actions**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Reopen** to reopen all the selected closed tickets, or click **Close ticket** to close all the replied tickets.
4. Review the warning message that is displayed to validate the action.
5. Click **Yes** to confirm the action.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

A notification message is displayed confirming the action.
