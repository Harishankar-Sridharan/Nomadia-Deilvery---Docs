# Missions

Use these pages to create, configure, and manage missions in Nomadia Delivery.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to missions.
* Identify whether you need to create, configure, or manage missions, then choose the matching page below.

## Feature overview

* **Adding a Mission**: Provides a single-page mission creation experience that brings mission parameters, constraints, and dependencies together.
* **Bulk Edit Mission Data**: Enables large-scale updates to mission information, reducing manual effort.
* **Custom Fields**: Covers creating custom fields while importing, inheriting data from contractors and address lists, mission visibility, and grouping.
* **Customizing Status Labels**: Lets you configure status labels and colors to align with your business workflows.
* **Automatic Priority via Delivery Dates**: Sets the priority of a mission to Highest based on its commitment dates.
* **Cross Docking Missions**: Maintains the same mission ID (Barcode) across mid-mile and last-mile logistics.
* **Drop Shipping Missions**: Lets transporters deliver parcels directly from suppliers to end customers, without returning to the agency.
