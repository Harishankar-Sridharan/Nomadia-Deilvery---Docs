# Change the order of mission status shortcuts

Change the order of the mission status shortcuts on the Missions page so they appear in your preferred sequence.

## Change the order of mission status shortcuts

1. Click the **Filter configuration icon (grey panel)**.
2. Drag mission statuses into the preferred sequence.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (178).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Save** or apply changes (for new filter creation only).
