# View a Mission information

View the details of a mission from the Missions page.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to the **Missions** page.
* Identify the mission you want to view in the missions table.

## Feature overview

* **Pen icon**: Opens the details panel for the corresponding mission.
* **Details panel**: Shows information such as pickup details, delivery location, parcel barcode and contents, and contact information.

## View a mission information

1. Click the **Pen** icon corresponding to the desired mission.

### Result

A details panel opens showing information such as:

* **Pickup details**
* **Delivery location**
* **Parcel barcode and contents**
* **Contact information, etc.**
