# Refresh KPI date and Mission statuses

This feature allows users to manually refresh the mission KPIs and statistics displayed on the home page. It ensures that the most recent data is retrieved and shown, providing users with up-to-date insights into their mission progress and performance.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** and open the **Home Page**.

## Feature overview

* **Refresh KPIs icon**: Located at the top-left section of the Home Page, next to the Last Updated Date and Time; updates the displayed data.
* **Last Updated Date and Time**: Shows when the displayed data was last updated.

## Refresh the KPIs

1. From the **Home Page**, navigate to the top-left section of the screen.
2. Locate the **Refresh KPIs** icon positioned next to the Last Updated Date and Time.
3. Click the **Refresh the KPIs** icon to update the displayed data with the latest available information.
