# parenttochildinheritance

The **parenttochildinheritance** feature automatically synchronizes settings from a parent container to its child containers. This eliminates the need to manually update each individual child container. Enable this to save time and ensure data consistency across your fleet.

### Getting Started

Prerequisites and system requirements:
* Active account in **Nomadia Delivery** with **Configuration** panel access.
* Parent and child containers configured with correct identifiers.

Steps:
1. Navigate to the **Configuration** menu.

   ![Flow 0:11 to 0:27](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_11_to_0_to_27.gif "Configuration Navigation – Navigating to container types and finding the correct identifier.")

2. Click on **Container Types**.

### Feature Overview

* **Inherit sub status to child machines**: This toggle switch automatically passes parent container status to child containers.

  ![Frame at 0:39](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_39.png "Inherit sub status to child machines – Toggle to enable status inheritance from parent to child containers.")

* **Palette**: This icon opens configuration options for a specific container type.

  ![Frame at 0:34](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_34.png "Palette – Icon to open configuration details for a container.")

* **Pencil icon**: This edit button opens the configuration mode for a machine.

  ![Frame at 1:06](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_1_to_06.png "Pencil icon – Edit button to modify machine details.")

* **Inherit attributes to child machines**: This setting automatically copies parent details to child containers.

  ![Frame at 3:07](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_3_to_07.png "Inherit attributes to child machines – Checkbox to enable address and details inheritance.")

* **Inherit documents to child machines**: This option automatically shares parent documents with child containers.

  ![Frame at 4:24](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_24.png "Inherit documents to child machines – Toggle to enable PDF and document inheritance.")

### How To: Enable and Inherit Machine Sub Statuses

1. Navigate to the **Configuration** menu.

   ![Flow 0:11 to 0:27](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_11_to_0_to_27.gif "Configuration Navigation – Navigating to container types and finding the correct identifier.")

2. Click on **Container Types**.
3. Scroll down to see the correct identifier.
4. Click the **Palette** icon.

   ![Flow 0:27 to 0:34](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_27_to_0_to_34.gif "Open Palette – Locating the correct identifier and opening its configuration.")

5. Toggle **Inherit sub status to child machines** to **Yes**.

   ![Frame at 0:39](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_39.png "Enable Sub-Status – Setting the inheritance toggle to Yes.")

6. Click on the **Save** button.

   ![Frame at 0:51](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_51.png "Save Configuration – Clicking save to store the setting.")

7. Go to **Machines**.
8. Click the **Pencil icon** next to the parent container machine number.

   ![Flow 0:51 to 1:06](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_0_to_51_to_1_to_06.gif "Open Machine Edit – Opening edit mode for the parent container.")

9. Change the **Machine status**.

   ![Frame at 1:16](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_1_to_16.png "Modify Status – Choosing a new machine status for the parent.")

10. Click on **Save**.

    ![Frame at 1:38](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_1_to_38.png "Save Machine Status – Applying status update to parent and child machines.")

### How To: Select and Inherit Custom Machine Statuses

1. Go to **Configuration**.
2. Click **Container Types**.
3. Scroll down to see the current identifier.

   ![Flow 1:42 to 2:07](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_1_to_42_to_2_to_07.gif "Configuration Navigation – Locating current identifier in container types.")

4. Select specific child status options like **Out for delivery** and **To be loaded**.

   ![Frame at 2:12](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_2_to_12.png "Select Child Statuses – Choosing status options for child containers.")

5. Click the **Save button**.

   ![Frame at 2:19](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_2_to_19.png "Save Mappings – Saving the selected child status mappings.")

6. Go to **Machines**.
7. Click the **Parent container**.

   ![Flow 2:25 to 2:34](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_2_to_25_to_2_to_34.gif "Open Parent Container – Navigating to parent container under Machines.")

8. Select the status configured in container types, such as **Out for delivery**.

   ![Frame at 2:39](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_2_to_39.png "Select Parent Status – Applying the predefined status to parent.")

9. Click on **Save**.

   ![Frame at 2:49](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_2_to_49.png "Save Selection – Saving and verifying automatic status propagation.")

### How To: Inherit Attributes to Child Containers

1. Navigate to **Configuration**.
2. Click on **Container Types**.
3. Toggle **Inherit attributes to child machines** to enable it.

   ![Frame at 3:07](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_3_to_07.png "Enable Attributes Inheritance – Toggling inheritance for child container attributes.")

4. Click on **Save**.

   ![Frame at 3:14](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_3_to_14.png "Save Attributes Setup – Saving configuration changes.")

5. Go to **Machines**.
6. Click the **Parent container**.

   ![Flow 3:14 to 3:38](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_3_to_14_to_3_to_38.gif "Open Parent Container – Accessing parent container details.")

7. Correct the **Address** if the address is wrong.

   ![Frame at 3:53](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_3_to_53.png "Edit Address – Correcting address information on the parent container.")

8. Click on **Save**.

   ![Frame at 4:08](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_08.png "Save Address – Saving parent address which automatically propagates to child containers.")

### How To: Inherit Documents to Child Containers

1. Navigate to **Configuration**.
2. Click on **Container Types**.
3. Toggle **Inherit documents to child machines** to enable it.

   ![Frame at 4:24](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_24.png "Enable Documents Inheritance – Toggling document inheritance for child containers.")

4. Click on **Save**.

   ![Frame at 4:26](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_26.png "Save Document Setup – Saving document inheritance configuration.")

5. Go to **Machines**.
6. Click the **Parent container**.

   ![Flow 4:30 to 4:44](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_30_to_4_to_44.gif "Open Parent Container – Navigating to parent container details.")

7. Click **Browse the computer**.
8. Select the **PDF** file.

   ![Flow 4:44 to 4:57](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_4_to_44_to_4_to_57.gif "Browse and Select Document – Choosing a PDF from computer files.")

9. Click the **Save button**.

   ![Frame at 5:09](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_5_to_09.png "Save Document – Uploading and saving the PDF document on parent container.")

#### Troubleshooting: Unsaved Changes Warning

If you attempt to go back without saving after uploading documents, a pop-up window will display.

1. Click **No** on the pop-up window to stay on the page.

   ![Frame at 5:19](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_5_to_19.png "Unsaved Changes Warning – Confirmation dialog warning about leaving without saving.")

2. Click the **Save button** to complete the upload.

   ![Frame at 5:23](parenttochildinheritance-parenttochildinheritance_timestamp_5_to_23.png "Confirm Save – Saving changes to prevent data loss.")

### Productivity Tips

* 💡 **Automatic Syncing**: Updating parent container status, address, or documents automatically updates child containers.
* ⚠️ **Avoid Unsaved Documents**: Do not click the back button before saving uploaded PDF files to prevent data loss.
