# Email

Enable the outgoing email service so that Nomadia Delivery can send email notifications to customers.

## Enable email notification

1. Go to **Configuration**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (105) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click **Configuration Menu**.
3. Under **Fulfillment and Client Experience**, click **Configure Outgoing Messages**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (107) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Select **General Configuration**.
5. Under **Configure Outgoing Emails to the Customers**, enable **Customers Outgoing Email Service**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (108) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (211).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
