# SMS

Enable the outgoing SMS service so that Nomadia Delivery can send SMS notifications to customers.

## Enable SMS notifications

1. Go to **Configuration**.
2. Click **Configuration Menu**.
3. Under **Fulfillment and Client Experience**, click **Configure Outgoing Messages**.
4. Select **General Configuration**.
5. Under **Configure Outgoing SMS to the Customers**, enable **Customers Outgoing SMS Service**.
6. Click **Save**.
