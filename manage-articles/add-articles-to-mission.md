# Add Articles to Mission

Add articles to a mission from the Mission page so the products that are part of the delivery are tracked with the mission.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **Nomadia Delivery** with a user profile that has access to the Mission page.
* Have the **Article name** and **Planned quantity** of each article available.

## Feature overview

* **Articles**: Tab where you add the products that are part of the delivery.
* **Add Articles**: Button that opens the form to enter the article information.
* **Article name**: Required information that identifies the article.
* **Planned quantity**: Required information that states the quantity planned for the mission.

## Add articles to a mission

1. From the Mission page, click **Actions**.
2. Select **Add** from the dropdown menu.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (34) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Articles**.
4. Click **Add Articles**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (35) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Enter the required information, including the **Article name** and **Planned quantity**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (36) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Click **Add**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (37) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

The articles are added successfully.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (38) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
