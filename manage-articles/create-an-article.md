# Create an Article

Create an article type to define a kind of article that you can use in the delivery process.

## Create an article type

1. Go to **Configuration**.
2. Click the **Configuration** menu.
3. Under Customization, click **Types of Articles.**
4. Click the **Actions** dropdown menu.
5. Click **Add**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (21) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Enter the **Name** and **Translation**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (23) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (24) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

The article type is created successfully.
