# Validate routes

This page explains how to validate the routes you want to use.

## When to use it

Validate the routes when the plan looks right. Validating dispatches the routes. Save the simulation first, then validate the routes.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Select the check boxes of the routes you want to validate.
3. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow, and then click **Validate**.

You can also click **Validate routes** in the **Optimization results** window.

## Result

The validated routes appear in the **Validated routes** panel, with their **Route**, **Date** and **Deliverer**. See [View validated routes](simulation-validated-routes.md).
