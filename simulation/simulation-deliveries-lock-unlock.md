# Lock and unlock deliveries

This page explains how to lock deliveries to keep them as they are, and how to unlock them again.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Deliveries**.
2. Select the check boxes of the deliveries you want to lock or unlock.
3. Click the **Deliveries** drop-down arrow.
4. Click **Lock** to lock the deliveries, or **Unlock** to unlock them.

## Result

The **Locked** column of the **Deliveries** panel shows whether a delivery is locked.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Locked routes**: Some actions, such as **Single route ordering**, have no effect when they only concern locked routes.
{% endhint %}
