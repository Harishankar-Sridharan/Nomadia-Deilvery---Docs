# Delete routes

This page explains how to remove the selected routes from the simulation.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Select the check boxes of the routes you want to delete.
3. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow, and then click **Delete**.

## Result

The routes are removed from the list in the **Routes** panel.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Keep your changes**: Click **Save changes** in the toolbar to keep the changes you made to the simulation.
{% endhint %}
