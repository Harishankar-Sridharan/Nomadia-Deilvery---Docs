# Reverse routes

This page explains how to reverse the order of the stops on the selected routes.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Select the check boxes of the routes you want to reverse.
3. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow, and then click **Reverse**.

To reverse a single route, open the route and click **Reverse** at the top right of the panel. See [View a route](simulation-routes-view-route.md).

## Result

You can check the new order in the route view.
