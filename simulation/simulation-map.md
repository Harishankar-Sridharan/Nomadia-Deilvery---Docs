# View the map

This page explains how to use the **Map** panel to see where your deliveries and routes are.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Map**.
2. Click **Navigation** to move around the map.
3. Click **Selection** to select deliveries on the map.
4. Click **Objects visibility** to choose what the map shows. See [Choose what the map shows](simulation-map-objects-visibility.md).
5. Click **Graphical information** to see more details on the map. See [Use graphical information](simulation-map-graphical-information.md).
6. Type an address in the search box to find a location.
7. Use the zoom-in and zoom-out buttons to change the zoom level.

![The Map panel with colored routes, numbered delivery markers and the map tools](../.gitbook/assets/Simulation_Map_001.png)

## Features

* [Choose what the map shows](simulation-map-objects-visibility.md): Show or hide routes, deliveries and vehicles, and choose a map style.
* [Use graphical information](simulation-map-graphical-information.md): Turn on coloring or density on the map.

## Result

The map shows each route in its own color. Numbered markers show the order of the deliveries on each route.
