# Customize the routes list

This page explains how to choose the columns of the **Routes** panel and change the route colors.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow.
3. Click **Customize the list** to choose your columns, or **Coloring** to change the route colors.

## Result

The **Routes** panel shows your columns and colors.
