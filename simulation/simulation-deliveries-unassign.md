# Unassign deliveries

This page explains how to remove the selected deliveries from the routes they are assigned to.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Deliveries**.
2. Select the check boxes of the deliveries you want to unassign.
3. Click the **Deliveries** drop-down arrow, and then click **Unassign**.

## Result

You can check the deliveries in the **Planned** and **Assigned to** columns of the **Deliveries** panel.
