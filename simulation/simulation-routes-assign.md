# Assign routes

This page explains how to assign the selected routes.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Select the check boxes of the routes you want to assign.
3. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow, and then click **Assign**.

You can also select a deliverer for a route directly in the **Mobile user** list of the route.

## Result

You can check the assignment in the **Mobile user** column of the **Routes** panel.
