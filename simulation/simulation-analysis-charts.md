# Analysis charts

This page explains what each chart of the **Analysis** panel shows.

## Before you begin

* Add the **Analysis** panel to the page. See [Analyze the results](simulation-analysis.md).

## Steps

1. Click the **Analysis** drop-down arrow.
2. Click one of the following charts:
   * **Comparison of availability of vehicles with the cumulation of the durations of delivery (planned/unplanned)**: compares the availability of the vehicles with the total delivery durations, for planned and unplanned deliveries.
   * **Number of deliveries by duration**: shows how many deliveries there are for each duration.
   * **Sum by delivery duration**: shows the sum for each delivery duration.
   * **Number of delivery frequencies**: shows how many deliveries there are for each frequency.
   * **Number of deliveries by time window**: shows how many deliveries there are for each time window.
   * **Number of deliveries by city**: shows how many deliveries there are in each city.
   * **Number of deliveries by territory**: shows how many deliveries there are in each territory.
   * **Usage rate by vehicles**: shows how much each vehicle is used.

## Result

The panel shows the chart you selected. Charts show **Planned deliveries** and **Unplanned deliveries** in different colors.
