# Customize the deliveries list

This page explains how to choose the columns of the **Deliveries** panel, change the colors of the deliveries and build your own filters.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Choose the columns

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Deliveries**.
2. Click the **Deliveries** drop-down arrow, and then click **Customize the list**.
3. On the **Columns** tab, select one or more fields in **Available fields**, and then click the right arrow to move them to **Displayed fields**.
4. To remove a column, select it in **Displayed fields**, and then click the left arrow.
5. To change the order of the columns, select a field in **Displayed fields**, and then click the up or the down arrow.
6. Optional: turn on **Text display mode** to select **Fit to data**.
7. Click **Save**.

Use the **Footer** tab to set the footer of the table. Click **Cancel** to close the window without saving.

![The Customize the list window with the available and the displayed fields](../.gitbook/assets/Simulation_DeliveriesCustomizeList_001.jpg)

## Change the colors

1. Click the **Deliveries** drop-down arrow, and then click **Coloring**.
2. In the **Deliveries color** window, each line is a rule. A rule has a condition, for example **Assigned to = "Deliverer-James"**, and a color.
3. Select the check box of a rule to use it, or click the trash icon to delete it.
4. Click **Add** to create a new rule.
5. To show the colors in the schedule, turn on **Apply the coloring in the schedule**.
6. Click **Save**.

![The Deliveries color window with the coloring rules](../.gitbook/assets/Simulation_DeliveriesCustomizeList_002.jpg)

## Filter the list

* Type a value in a column's **Filter…** box, and click the comparison operator next to it to change how the filter works.
* Click **Create your custom filters by clicking here** to build a custom filter.
* Click **Hide the filter row** to hide the filter boxes.

## Result

The **Deliveries** panel shows your columns, colors and filters.
