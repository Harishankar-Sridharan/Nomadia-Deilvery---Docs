# Accessing eCMR

An eCMR (Electronic Consignment Note) is the digital equivalent of the traditional paper-based CMR used in road transport. It acts as proof of the carriage contract, acknowledgment of goods received, and a document of title. You download the eCMR PDF directly through the delivery management system.

## Before you begin

* Configure the company name, address, and SIRET number in **Configuration → General** before generating the eCMR. These details are required by law and are displayed on the eCMR.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (125) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Feature overview

* **eCMR (Electronic Consignment Note)**: The digital equivalent of the paper-based CMR used in road transport. It acts as proof of the carriage contract, acknowledgment of goods received, and a document of title.
* **Configuration → General**: The place where you configure the company name, address, and SIRET number that appear on the eCMR.
* **Actions → Consignment notes**: Generates the eCMR for the routes you select on the **Missions** tab.
* **Download** button: Downloads the eCMR PDF.

## Download the eCMR

1. Log in to the delivery management system and go to the **Missions** tab.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (124) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Select the routes for which you want to generate the eCMR.
3. Click **Actions → Consignment notes**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (126) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click the **Download** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (128) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Result

The system generates the eCMR PDF, which you can download and share with deliverers.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (129) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Tips

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Required company details**: Configure the company name, address, and SIRET number in **Configuration → General** before you generate the eCMR. These details are required by law and are displayed on the eCMR.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Share with deliverers**: After the system generates the eCMR PDF, you can download it and share it with deliverers.
{% endhint %}
